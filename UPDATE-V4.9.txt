Rumble Train Blood Bowl Team Builder V4.9

PRINT PAGE 1 — TEAM EXTRAS REWORK
---------------------------------
Team Re-rolls is now its own full-width section.

Each of the following now has its own separate printable section:
- Apothecary
- Assistant Coaches
- Cheerleaders
- Dedicated Fans

The four sideline sections are arranged in a 2 x 2 grid so they remain
prominent while still fitting comfortably on the same A4 landscape page.

Everything else from V4.8 is unchanged:
- larger roster summary boxes
- sports-style Graduate display font
- Page 2 Skills & Traits Reference
- portable Save/Load Team files
- player/team name generators
- SPP and Injury print columns

3. Commit to main.
